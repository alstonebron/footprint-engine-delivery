Golden Memorial: 20-Section Blueprint

Project Overview: Golden Memorial is a multi-generational, family-owned practice. They needed a presence that honored the weight of the work without feeling cold or corporate. We delivered a twenty-section brand and presence blueprint — voice, structure, page-by-page — that the family approved on first review.

Working with Footprint was the easiest yes we’ve had in years. They listened, they wrote, they delivered. The blueprint read like they’d been in the family for a decade.

Objectives

  1. Capture a voice that is warm, dignified, and clear — never salesy.
  2. Structure twenty sections of content so families can find what they need in a hard moment.
  3. Give the family a blueprint they can grow with for the next decade.

What we shipped

  1. Voice and Messaging System:
  • A written voice guide the whole family can use, from the website to the printed materials.
  • Language that is direct and human, never clinical and never saccharine.
  1. Twenty-Section Site Blueprint:
  • Every section mapped: what it says, who it serves, what action it invites.
  • Approved on first review — no revisions requested.
  1. Local Search and Maps Foundation:
  • Tuned for the city and the surrounding communities they serve.
  • Built so the right families find them first, at the moment it matters.
  1. Owned Outright:
  • Every word, every page, every asset belongs to Golden Memorial.
  • Full export rights, day one.

Outcome

Golden Memorial now has a presence that matches the care they put into the work. The blueprint guides every piece of communication they ship, and the family finally feels like the outside of the practice matches the inside.
