Unleashing the Power of a Clear Position

January 2, 2024

Note: This post is part of the Footprint writing archive — clear thinking on local growth, presence, and owned systems.

In the ever-evolving world of local marketing, staying on top of the latest trends and tools is essential. One principle that has gained immense importance in recent years is the clear position — the single sentence that everything else hangs on.

What Is a Clear Position?

A clear position is a one-sentence answer to three questions: what you do, who you do it for, and why it matters. Not “I’m a consultant” but “I help retired teachers in Portland keep more of what they saved.” Specific, memorable, and impossible to confuse with anyone else.

Why It Matters

Without a clear position, every marketing decision is a guess. What should your homepage say? What should you post about? Who should you network with? A clear position answers all of these — and makes every subsequent decision easier.

How to Find Yours

  1. Look at your best clients: What do they have in common? What problem did you solve for them? What did they say about the work?

  2. Look at your best work: What projects are you proudest of? What do they have in common?

  3. Look at your market: What’s missing? What are competitors saying that you can say better — or more specifically?

  4. Write the sentence: One sentence. What you do, who you do it for, why it matters. Test it on a stranger. If they can repeat it back, you’ve got it.

The Compounding Effect

A clear position compounds. Every page you write, every post you publish, every conversation you have reinforces it. Over time, you become the obvious choice for the specific people you serve — and the practice grows without you having to push.

Unleashing the power of a clear position isn’t about being clever. It’s about being clear — and letting that clarity do the heavy lifting.
