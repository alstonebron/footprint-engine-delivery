Unveiling the Magic: The 6-Phase Growth System

October 11, 2023

Note: This post is part of the Footprint writing archive — clear thinking on local growth, presence, and owned systems.

Most marketing fails because the pieces don’t have jobs. The Footprint growth system assigns every piece a job and runs the same six phases every time, so the results are repeatable — not a roll of the dice. Here’s how each phase works.

Phase 1: Clarify

Make your value obvious. If a stranger can’t repeat what you do in one sentence, nothing else matters. This phase is about finding that sentence and hanging everything on it.

Phase 2: Build Trust

A presence that matches your work. The outside of the practice should read like the inside — considered, confident, free of noise. This phase is about closing the gap between how good you are and how good you look.

Phase 3: Create Demand

The right people find you first. Tuned to your city, your specialty, your actual clients. This phase is about building a local search and content foundation that compounds.

Phase 4: Convert Interest

Attention into calls and bookings. Every page, every post, every video ends in a clear next step. This phase is about removing friction and making it easy for the right person to raise their hand.

Phase 5: Follow Up

No lead slips through. Automated, on-brand, and relentless — without being annoying. This phase is about building a system that follows up so you don’t have to.

Phase 6: Optimize

Real data, no guessing. Watch what works, do more of it, cut what doesn’t. This phase is about making the system smarter every month.

Why It Works

The system is boring on purpose. It runs the same way every time, so the results compound instead of resetting every quarter. No black boxes, no magic — just six phases, each with a job, each doing it.

The magic isn’t in any one phase. It’s in the system — and in the fact that it runs whether you’re watching or not.
